Procedure

How a test works

  1. Read the termsTripwire pulls the promises out of the offering document: lock-ups, who may buy, holder caps and blocked countries.
  2. Attack the tokenFor every promise it tries the move that should be illegal, from direct transfers to approved spenders.
  3. File the reportEach gap is stamped, with the legal sentence beside the line of code that contradicts it.

Beneficiaries

Who it protects

InvestorsLearn whether the rules that protect them are real before they buy.
Issuers and their lawyersCatch a mismatch before launch, not in a regulator’s letter.
Regulators and auditorsGet evidence of enforcement instead of a promise.

Test a real token

    The same attacks run on a real EVM chain, with transaction hashes in the reports. This page replays them on a simulated model of the token. Tripwire tests whether code enforces the terms. It does not judge whether the terms are legally sufficient and is not legal advice. Findings describe what the code does under test conditions and are a starting point for the issuer’s review, not an accusation or a legal conclusion.
