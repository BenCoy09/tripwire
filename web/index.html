The legal terms say one thing. The code does another.

Tripwire reads the legal terms behind a tokenized asset, then attacks the token to find out which promises the code actually keeps.

See it break a tokenView the code

Run on a real EVM chain: the deployed token held 1 of 4 promises. The fixed token held 4 of 4.

How it works

Three steps, no blockchain knowledge needed to follow the result.

  1. Read the termsTripwire pulls the promises out of the offering document: lock-ups, who may buy, holder caps and blocked countries.
  2. Attack the tokenFor every promise it tries the move that should be illegal, from direct transfers to approved spenders.
  3. See the gapEach breach shows the legal sentence beside the line of code that contradicts it.

Who it protects

InvestorsLearn whether the rules that protect them are real before they buy.
Issuers and their lawyersCatch a mismatch before launch, not in a regulator’s letter.
Regulators and auditorsGet evidence of enforcement instead of a promise.
